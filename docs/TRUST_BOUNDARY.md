# Trust Boundary

Trust is a record-preservation and stewardship instrument. It is not a legal-status engine.

## Governing laws

- `TRUST RECORD != TRUST CREATION`
- `RECORDED AUTHORITY != LEGAL AUTHORITY`
- `EXEMPTION CLAIM != EXEMPTION ESTABLISHED`

## Recorded state

Trust may preserve:

- governing instruments and amendments;
- trustee designation and recorded acceptance conduct;
- powers and limitations stated in a carrier;
- asset/title carriers and custody;
- decisions, approvals, recusals, conflicts, and reversals;
- receipts, expenditures, distributions, transfers, and reimbursements;
- filing and exemption positions plus acknowledgments.

It should describe these as recorded facts or attributed positions: **designated**, **accepted by recorded conduct**, **claimed**, **filed**, **acknowledged**, **executed according to recorded carrier**, **disputed**, or **unknown**.

## What storage does not do

Creating an object inside Trust does not itself:

- create a legally valid trust;
- transfer title;
- appoint a legally effective trustee;
- prove a person's powers;
- satisfy signature, oath, notarization, recording, or filing requirements;
- establish federal or state tax exemption;
- establish a Minnesota charitable-trust exemption.

Certificate-ready output is a data packet for later human/legal execution, not an official certificate merely because the app generated it.
