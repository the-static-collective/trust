# Trust Boundary

Trust records trust-related facts, instruments, claims, actions, and receipts. Storage is not legal creation.

## Laws

- **TRUST RECORD != TRUST CREATION**
- **RECORDED AUTHORITY != LEGAL AUTHORITY**
- **EXEMPTION CLAIM != EXEMPTION ESTABLISHED**
- **RENDERING != AUTHORITY**

## Descriptive states

Use states such as:

- designated / accepted / resigned / removed;
- proposed / approved / executed / reversed / disputed;
- claimed_exempt / registered / filing_recorded / unknown;
- draft_data_only.

Do not translate these into stronger conclusions than the carriers and applicable law establish.

## Certificate-ready data

Trust can preserve the data needed to prepare a Minnesota certificate-of-trust packet: trust identity, instrument date, empowered trustees, powers/limitations, number required to act, and termination/revocation state.

A `CertificateReadyPacket` is structurally marked `draft_data_only`. The application must not imply that the packet itself satisfies oath, notarization, recording, execution, or reliance requirements.

## Formation and authority

Governing instruments, property transfers/declarations, trustee designations, acceptance evidence, amendments, resolutions, filings, and acknowledgments are carriers/relations. Their presence improves record integrity; it does not let the application pronounce legal validity.
