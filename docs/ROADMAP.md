# Roadmap

Trust grows by crossings. Genesis proves the protocol-to-mobile seam first; later crossings add capability without retroactively pretending it existed earlier.

## Crossing 1 — Public Protocol Genesis

**Current.** Public Moment/provenance schemas, synthetic child-first fixture, Minnesota trust record baseline, minimal Expo shell, and CI.

Success condition: `public protocol → synthetic Moment → native renderer`.

## Crossing 2 — Native Capture + Encrypted Local Vault

Photo, video, audio, note, document/share-sheet intake; original-byte preservation before narration; SHA-256 capture receipts; app-controlled storage; SQLite/SQLCipher evaluation; secure key storage.

## Crossing 3 — Life Graph

People, Moments, plans versus occurrences, observer accounts, child statements, ordinary routines, and aligned/divergent perspective maps.

## Crossing 4 — Scrapbook

Timeline, cards, albums, child pages, memory-safe captions, audio memories, and provenance affordances that stay available without dominating normal family use.

## Crossing 5 — Trust Ledger

Governing instruments, trustee tenure, authority events, assets, restrictions, transactions, decisions, conflicts, disclosures, compliance positions, and certificate-ready draft data.

## Crossing 6 — Shared Reality

Multi-person/device synchronization with perspective ownership, non-destructive merge, shared Moment identity, disagreement visualization, and privacy boundaries.

## Crossing 7 — Export Doors

Scrapbook export, child-future archive, trust ledger, accounting packet, provenance bundle, narrow evidence chronology, and certificate-ready trust packet. Every export declares its source graph, transformation, privacy policy, and authority boundary.

## Stop rule

Do not pull cloud sync, AI composition, full accounting, or legal classification into an earlier crossing merely because the later roadmap already names them.
