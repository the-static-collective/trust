# Roadmap

Trust is built in seven crossings. Genesis implements the protocol/mobile proof; later crossings remain intentionally unclaimed.

## Crossing 1 — Public Protocol Genesis

**Current.** Define Moment, Carrier, CaptureReceipt, accounts/direct statements, privacy/audience primitives, Minnesota trust-administration record types, synthetic fixtures, conformance tests, and a minimal native renderer.

Success: one synthetic Moment validates through the public protocol and renders in the mobile shell without any private Black Star dependency.

## Crossing 2 — Native Capture + Encrypted Local Vault

Photo, note, audio, video, document/share-sheet intake; immutable original copy; SHA-256 capture receipt; crash-safe local persistence; encrypted structured storage and secure key handling.

Success: original bytes are protected before narration.

## Crossing 3 — Life Graph

People, Moment relations, plans versus occurrences, observer accounts, direct child statements, routines, and aligned/divergent perspective maps.

Success: differing accounts coexist without last-write-wins truth.

## Crossing 4 — Scrapbook

Timeline, Moment cards, albums, child pages, memory-safe captions, audio memories, and provenance affordances.

Success: normal use feels like a family-memory product rather than evidence software.

## Crossing 5 — Trust Ledger

Governing instruments, trustee tenure, authority events, assets, restrictions, transactions, decisions, conflicts, disclosures, compliance positions, and certificate-ready data packets.

Success: Minnesota record-preservation needs are representable without claiming legal validity.

## Crossing 6 — Shared Reality

Multi-person/device synchronization, perspective ownership, non-destructive merge, shared Moment identity, disagreement visualization, and privacy boundaries.

Success: synchronization preserves distinct accounts instead of resolving disagreement by overwrite.

## Crossing 7 — Export Doors

Scrapbook, child-future archive, trust ledger, accounting packet, provenance bundle, narrow evidence chronology, and certificate-ready trust data packet.

Success: every export declares its source graph, transformation, privacy policy, and authority boundary.
